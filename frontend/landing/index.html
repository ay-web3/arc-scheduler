Frequently Asked Questions

How does Arc Scheduler work?
Arc Scheduler allows you to schedule USDC payments on Arc Testnet by selecting a recipient, amount, and execution time. The smart contract and automated executor handle the rest.
Where are funds stored?
Funds are locked inside an on-chain escrow smart contract until execution or cancellation.
Who can execute payments?
The autonomous bot, the sender, or the recipient can trigger execution.
Can I cancel scheduled payments?
Yes. Any scheduled payment can be canceled before execution and refunded automatically.
Is my money safe?
Yes. Your funds are secured in the smart contract and can only be executed as scheduled or refunded if you cancel the payment.
Do I need to stay online for payments to execute?
No. The automated executor will execute your payments at the scheduled time even if you are offline.
How do Telegram notifications work?
After connecting your wallet, click “Enable Telegram Alerts” and press Start in the bot. You will receive notifications when payments are scheduled and executed or cancelled.
